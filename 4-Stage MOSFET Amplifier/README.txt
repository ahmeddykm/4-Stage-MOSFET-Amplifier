\f0\fs24 \cf0 4-Stage MOSFET Amplifier\
Design Project | April 2026\
\
I designed a 3.3 V MOSFET amplifier for sensor and mixed-signal applications using KiCad and LTspice\
.\
\
The design uses three cascaded common source gain stages followed by a source follower output stage.\
\
The design was simulated in LTSpice and was found to have an unloaded gain of 60.9 dB, a gain of 60.32 dB with a 10 k\uc0\u937  load, a bandwidth of approximately 45 MHz when driven through a 10 k\u937  resistor, a power consumption of less than 1 mW, and a output swing of 1.61 VPP.\
\
The design was verified to have correct DC biasing, AC frequency response, transient performance, and operation with a 10 k\uc0\u937  load using SPICE simulations.}
